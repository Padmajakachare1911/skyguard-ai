## 3. UAS Category

The applicable UAS category will be determined after the final drone configuration and maximum all-up weight, including payload, are confirmed.

The project team will verify the applicable DGCA classification before conducting real-world flight operations.

## 4. Current Compliance Status

The SkyGuard AI hardware configuration is currently being finalized.

Therefore, the compliance items in this document are maintained as **Pending / To Verify** until the relevant hardware, drone configuration, documentation, permissions and operational requirements are confirmed.

## 6. Pre-Flight Compliance Checks

Before any future field flight, the project team will verify:

- Applicable drone registration/type-certification requirements
- Applicable remote-pilot requirements
- Airspace status
- VLOS requirements
- Weather and visibility conditions
- Drone physical condition
- Battery and power-system condition
- Required safety equipment
- Safe operating area
- Distance from people and property
- Site permission
- Emergency procedures
- Required documentation
