## 4. Current Regulatory Status

The SkyGuard AI drone is currently in the project development and pre-hardware-integration stage.

The final drone configuration, maximum all-up weight, manufacturer/model details and applicable certification status have not yet been finalized.

Therefore, the UIN/registration status is currently marked as **Pending / To Verify**.

The project team will verify the latest applicable DGCA requirements before any real-world flight operation.

---

## 5. Important DGCA Note

As of the latest DGCA information reviewed for this project, DGCA states that registration of non-type-certified (non-TC) UAS has been temporarily suspended until further notice.

Therefore, the project team will not treat this document as proof of registration or as a UIN application.

The final regulatory position will be verified against the latest official DGCA requirements when the physical drone configuration is finalized.
